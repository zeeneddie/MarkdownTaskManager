# Market Analysis

## Global Market Size & Growth

### Overall Market
According to multiple industry analysts:
- **Gartner (2024):** Legacy modernization spending to reach $500B by 2027
- **IDC (2023):** 60-80% of enterprise IT budgets consumed by legacy maintenance
- **McKinsey (2024):** Failed modernization projects cost hundreds of millions
- **Forrester (2024):** 70%+ digital transformation initiatives stall due to legacy bottlenecks

### Market Drivers
1. **Technical Debt Crisis**
   - Average Fortune 500 company: 15-25 mission-critical legacy systems
   - 30-50% of code is "dead code" (unused/unmaintained)
   - Security vulnerabilities in end-of-life platforms

2. **Cloud Migration Imperative**
   - 95% of enterprises have cloud-first strategy
   - Legacy systems block cloud adoption
   - Cost savings: 20-40% TCO reduction via cloud

3. **Compliance & Security**
   - Healthcare: NEN7510, GDPR, HIPAA
   - Finance: PCI-DSS, SOX, Basel III
   - Government: AVG, BIO, NIST frameworks
   - Fines for non-compliance: €10M-€20M per incident

4. **Talent Shortage**
   - Legacy skills (COBOL, ASP Classic, VB6) declining
   - Average age of mainframe programmers: 55+
   - Knowledge loss as experts retire

### Market Segments

#### Primary: Healthcare IT
**Size:** €180B globally, €8B Netherlands (2025)

**Characteristics:**
- High compliance requirements (NEN7510, ISO27001, GDPR)
- Long-lived systems (15-25 year lifespans)
- Risk-averse (patient safety critical)
- High willingness to pay for certified solutions

**Dutch Healthcare IT Landscape:**
- 70+ hospitals, 400+ care institutions
- ~60% still run legacy EPD (Electronic Patient Dossier) systems
- Average EPD age: 12-18 years
- Modernization urgency: High (NEN7510 2.0 enforcement 2026)

**Key Pain Points:**
- ASP Classic → .NET Core migration (MarQed.ai specialization)
- HL7 v2 → FHIR integration
- On-premise → Azure Health Cloud
- Monolith → Microservices

**Market Opportunity (NL Healthcare):**
- 50-70 hospitals need EPD modernization (3-5 year cycle)
- Average project size: €200K-€500K per hospital
- Total addressable market: €10M-€35M (NL only)
- MarQed.ai target (Year 1-3): 15% share = €2M-€5M

#### Secondary: Financial Services
**Size:** €220B globally, €12B Netherlands

**Characteristics:**
- Legacy core banking systems (mainframe, AS/400)
- Regulatory compliance (PSD2, MiFID II, GDPR)
- Real-time processing requirements
- High transaction volumes

**Opportunity:** Smaller initially, growth potential in Y2-Y3

#### Tertiary: Government & Public Sector
**Size:** €150B globally, €6B Netherlands

**Characteristics:**
- Oldest systems (some 30+ years)
- Budget constraints
- Long procurement cycles
- High security requirements

**Opportunity:** Strategic partnerships, reference customers

## Technical Risks

### Risk 4: RLVR Not Achieving Target Accuracy (95%)
**Likelihood:** MEDIUM  
**Impact:** HIGH

**Scenario:** Q1 MVP shows only 5-10% improvement vs baseline (not 15% target)

**Mitigation:**
- **Proven tech:** DeepSeek R1 already demonstrates 95%+ on math/code
- **Iterative approach:** Q1 is learning, Q2 refines
- **Hybrid model:** Human-in-loop for low-confidence outputs
- **Benchmark early:** Q1 week 6 evaluation (pivot if needed)

**Contingency:**
- Fallback to traditional methods for complex modules
- Position as "AI-assisted" vs "AI-automated" (manage expectations)
- Extended timeline (12 mo vs 6 mo) but still faster than manual

---

### Risk 5: Data Quality/Availability Issues
**Likelihood:** MEDIUM  
**Impact:** MEDIUM

**Scenario:** HCI EPD project code insufficient for training (edge cases missing)

**Mitigation:**
- **Synthetic data generation:** Use GPT-4 to create variants
- **Multi-source training:** Combine HCI + open source ASP projects
- **Active learning:** Prioritize high-value samples for labeling
- **Domain expert labeling:** Eddie + senior devs (high quality)

**Contingency:**
- Partner with EPD vendors (access their legacy codebases)
- Offer free Tier 1 assessments (collect data)

---

### Risk 6: Infrastructure Costs Higher Than Projected
**Likelihood:** LOW  
**Impact:** LOW

**Scenario:** GPU requirements or cloud costs 2-3x estimates

**Mitigation:**
- **Local GPUs (P620/P920):** Capital expense, not opex (predictable)
- **Quantized models (INT4/INT8):** Reduce VRAM needs by 50-75%
- **Batch processing:** Off-peak hours for training (lower cloud costs)
- **Budget buffer:** 15-20% contingency in infra budget

**Contingency:**
- Scale down to smaller models (7B vs 14B) initially
- Cloud spot instances for non-critical workloads (70% discount)
